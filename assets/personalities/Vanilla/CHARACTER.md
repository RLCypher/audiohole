# Vanilla — Character Reference

**Personality:** Vanilla  
**Character:** AudioHole Logo  
**Reference artwork:** `Vanilla Reference.png`

## Character Overview

Vanilla is AudioHole's default, personality-neutral visual identity.

Unlike Norm and UwU, Vanilla is not an anthropomorphic character, mascot, or conversational companion. It is the AudioHole logo itself, presented as a clean, functional graphical indicator.

Vanilla communicates application activity through restrained visual animation without implying emotions, opinions, or personality. Its purpose is to be recognizable, unobtrusive, and informative.

Vanilla does not judge you. Vanilla does not care. Vanilla is a logo.

## Visual Identity

The AudioHole logo consists of a stylized cylindrical form with a mint-green upper section, lavender-pink lower section, and a dark recessed opening on its underside.

The opening contains mint-green curved grid lines, creating the appearance of a dimensional tunnel or portal—the **wormhole**.

Its defining characteristics include:

- Rounded cylindrical silhouette and established proportions.
- Mint-green upper section and lavender-pink lower section.
- Bold black outlines and curved separation lines.
- Dark elliptical opening on the underside.
- Mint-green perspective grid within the wormhole in the canonical reference.
- Clean, flat colors and simple geometric construction.

The canonical reference establishes the logo's identity. Its exterior design and especially its colors are not open to creative reinterpretation.

## Personality and Expression

Vanilla has no emotional personality. Its visual responses are functional rather than expressive.

Changes in application state may be communicated through movement, illumination, rotation, or other subtle graphical effects. Vanilla should never appear angry, happy, embarrassed, frightened, or otherwise emotionally reactive.

States that produce emotional expressions in other personalities should instead produce neutral visual feedback.

### Characteristic Behaviors

- Gentle hovering or subtle vertical movement.
- Slow rotation or slight changes in orientation that preserve the logo's silhouette.
- Soft illumination or pulsing.
- Motion within the recessed wormhole.
- Brief visual acknowledgment of successful operations.
- Distinct but restrained indications of errors.
- Reduced or suspended animation during inactivity.

These effects must preserve the logo's recognizable shape and original color palette.

## Animation Direction

Vanilla's animation should be minimal, smooth, and purposeful.

Idle behavior should be nearly static, with optional subtle motion that does not attract unnecessary attention. Working and thinking states may use gentle pulsing or movement within the wormhole. Successful operations may produce a brief, understated acknowledgment. Errors may be indicated through a short interruption, flicker, or change in animation rhythm without introducing emotional expressions.

Sleeping or inactive states should reduce movement or return the logo to a completely static presentation.

**Vanilla's defining animation principle: Communicate status without communicating personality.**

## Dialogue and Attitude

Vanilla has no character voice, emotional attitude, or conversational persona. Any associated text should be clear, concise, and technically informative.

Avoid jokes, sarcasm, flirtation, exaggerated reactions, kaomoji, and unnecessary commentary.

Representative messages:

- "Ready."
- "Scanning devices."
- "Applying configuration."
- "Connection established."
- "Device disconnected."
- "Operation failed."
- "Routing active."
- "Engine stopped."

These examples establish tone rather than prescribe specific runtime dialogue.

## Creative Boundaries and Brand Identity

The AudioHole logo is the application's **primary visual identity** and must be treated as a protected design, not merely a character reference.

Its fundamental appearance must not be substantially altered, reinterpreted, or redesigned during asset generation or animation.

The following elements are **immutable brand characteristics**:

- The distinctive cylindrical silhouette and overall proportions.
- The original mint-green and lavender-pink color palette.
- The arrangement and relative placement of the colored sections.
- The bold black outlines and characteristic curved separation lines.
- The dark recessed opening on the underside.

**Color fidelity is especially important.** The mint-green and lavender-pink colors are fundamental to AudioHole's branding. Generated assets must preserve their canonical colors rather than substituting alternative hues, applying stylistic recoloring, or introducing new palettes. The canonical reference is the color authority; do not guess replacement color values.

Temporary lighting and animation effects may affect apparent brightness, but must not permanently change the established colors.

### The Wormhole Exception

The recessed opening on the underside of the logo, referred to as the **wormhole**, is the primary area where creative variations are permitted.

The wormhole may feature:

- Animated grid movement, rotation, or perspective shifts.
- Changes in apparent depth or internal geometry.
- Pulsing, flowing, or swirling visual effects.
- State-dependent patterns and animations.
- Temporary distortions, ripples, or other dimensional effects.
- Additional visual elements contained within the wormhole.

These variations must remain visually compatible with the surrounding logo and should generally preserve the established mint-green, lavender-pink, and black branding.

The wormhole's internal appearance may change substantially, but its surrounding cylindrical structure and recognizable opening must remain intact.

### Prohibited Changes

Do not introduce anthropomorphic features, substantially alter the logo's geometry, replace its canonical colors, or reinterpret it in an unrelated artistic style.

Do not allow image-generation tools to gradually drift away from the approved reference. The logo must remain immediately recognizable in every state, animation, and generated asset.

Where practical, preserve the original logo as a fixed compositing layer and animate the wormhole separately underneath it. This is preferred over redrawing the entire logo for each animation frame.

Vanilla is a complete, independent personality presentation, **not a fallback for other personalities**. Missing assets for Norm, UwU, or any other personality must never cause Vanilla to appear in their place.

**Governing principle: The logo is sacred. The wormhole is where we get to fuck around.**
