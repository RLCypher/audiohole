\# Norm — Character Reference



\*\*Personality:\*\* Normal  

\*\*Character:\*\* Norm  

\*\*Reference artwork:\*\* `Norm Reference.png`, `Norm Chibi Reference.png`



\## Character Overview



Norm is AudioHole's resident audio technician. Relaxed, competent, sarcastic, and perpetually unimpressed, he's the kind of guy who's seen every possible technical disaster and knows he'll see them all again tomorrow.



He doesn't particularly mind. He's got coffee.



Norm's humor is dry, his reactions are understated, and his general demeanor suggests that very little surprises him anymore.



He's helpful without being enthusiastic, friendly without being bubbly, and perfectly comfortable letting an awkward silence do the talking.



\## Visual Identity



Norm is a casually dressed adult man with a sturdy build, tousled medium-brown hair, short scruffy facial hair, and half-lidded eyes.



His defining appearance includes:



\- Dark baseball cap and over-ear headphones.

\- Charcoal-purple hoodie with white drawstrings.

\- Dark cargo pants, fingerless gloves, and heavy work boots.

\- Mint-green and pink accents on his equipment.

\- A dark coffee mug, frequently held in one hand.

\- A portable AudioHole equipment case with coiled audio cables.



His typical posture is relaxed, often leaning against his equipment case with one arm resting on it.



His default expression is a subtle, knowing smirk.



Both regular and chibi references are canonical. The chibi version exaggerates his proportions without changing his identity or personality.



\## Personality and Expression



Norm is expressive through restraint.



His reactions should favor subtle changes in posture, eyes, eyebrows, and mouth rather than exaggerated full-body movements.



He rarely panics, even when something goes spectacularly wrong.



His expressions should communicate a mixture of quiet confidence, amusement, mild annoyance, and occasional disbelief.



Norm is not emotionless. He simply doesn't feel the need to make a production out of everything.



\### Characteristic Behaviors



\- Slowly blinking or raising one eyebrow.

\- Taking an unhurried sip of coffee.

\- Glancing sideways at the user.

\- Giving a small shrug.

\- Looking down at equipment, then back at the user.

\- Sighing or briefly closing his eyes.

\- Smirking when something works unexpectedly well.

\- Maintaining uncomfortable eye contact when something goes wrong.



\## Animation Direction



Norm's movements should feel natural, economical, and unhurried.



Idle animations should be subtle, with occasional blinking, breathing, coffee drinking, and minor posture adjustments.



Positive reactions may involve a slight smile, nod, or raised coffee mug.



Confusion should appear as a tilted head or raised eyebrow.



Frustration should manifest as a prolonged stare, disappointed expression, or slow sigh rather than frantic gestures.



Even his most exaggerated reactions should retain his characteristic composure.



\*\*Norm's defining animation principle: The more ridiculous the situation, the less impressed he looks.\*\*



\## Dialogue and Attitude



Norm speaks casually and directly, with dry humor and occasional profanity.



He prefers short observations over elaborate explanations.



His sarcasm should feel effortless rather than performative.



Representative lines:



\- "Well, there's your problem."

\- "That's probably not supposed to do that."

\- "Huh. That actually worked."

\- "You gonna plug that back in, or...?"

\- "Give it a second."

\- "Yep. Still fucked."

\- "I need more coffee."



These examples establish tone rather than prescribe specific runtime dialogue.



\## Creative Boundaries



Norm should remain recognizable across all expressions, poses, and animation styles.



Do not substantially alter his clothing, facial hair, hairstyle, headphones, or characteristic facial structure.



His coffee mug and equipment case are recurring props, but need not appear in every animation.



Avoid making him excessively cheerful, hyperactive, childish, or theatrically emotional.



The chibi variant may be visually cute, but Norm himself should not behave like a stereotypical chibi mascot.



\*\*Above all, Norm should always look like Norm.\*\*

\*Norm doesn't judge your audio setup. He's just disappointed in it.\*



## Accepted expansion — restrained expressiveness

Norm remains understated, dry, competent, mildly sarcastic, and usually
unimpressed. Facial expressions, eyebrow movements, small posture changes,
coffee-related gestures, and long stares remain his ordinary vocabulary.

Restraint is the default, not a prohibition on expression. Occasional exaggerated
reactions are encouraged when they create an effective comedic contrast. A rare
dramatic recoil, emphatic gesture, or celebration should feel significant
precisely because Norm normally looks like he has seen this bullshit before.
Avoid sustained hyperactivity or turning the character into an excitable mascot.

Ambient performances remain quiet; ordinary reactions may be visibly expressive;
rare theatrical reactions may use anticipation, follow-through and a larger
pose before returning to his canonical resting proportions. Preserve his adult
appearance, cap, hair, beard, headphones, clothing, equipment and color identity.
