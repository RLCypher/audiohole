## Practical consequence

No A/B/C backend winner is supported by measured latency or CPU data. Strategy A reduces I/O code but leaves clock control, identity, supervisor and diagnostics to AudioHole. Strategy B (direct I/O plus processing components) is technically feasible but inherits the exact numeric/quality limits of selected components. Strategy C gives control but transfers all lifecycle/format/scheduling work to AudioHole. See [comparison](BACKEND_COMPARISON.md) and [validation plan](EXPERIMENTS.md).
